# KAISERRRR Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction
KAISERRRR ("the Bot") is a Discord bot that provides moderation, welcome messages, music playback, server utility commands, and voice channel features. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot may process and how it is handled.

## 2. Information We May Process
Depending on the features enabled by server administrators, the Bot may process:

- Discord user IDs and server IDs to identify users and servers.
- Usernames and relevant message or interaction details when commands are used.
- Moderation records, such as warnings or ban information, if moderation features are enabled.
- Welcome and goodbye configuration settings.
- Voice channel IDs and preferences required for the 24/7 voice connection feature.
- Technical logs needed to troubleshoot errors and maintain the Bot.

The Bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment card details, or sensitive personal information.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information may be used to:
- Execute commands and provide requested features.
- Manage moderation actions and server settings.
- Maintain voice channel connections.
- Diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

## 4. Storage and Retention
Some settings or moderation records may be stored in a database to support the Bot's functionality. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature, troubleshooting, or legitimate operational needs. Actual retention depends on the Bot's implementation and configuration.

## 5. Sharing of Information
We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and, where applicable, hosting or music-related services required to operate the Bot. Such services may handle information under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Voice and Music Features
The Bot may join a configured Discord voice channel to provide music or maintain a voice connection. The Bot does not intentionally record or store voice conversations. Music-related services may receive search queries or track identifiers when music commands are used.

## 7. Security
Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information and credentials. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Choices and Data Requests
Server administrators can disable or remove the Bot from their server. If you have a question or a request regarding information associated with your Discord account, contact the Bot's owner using the contact method provided on the Bot's support server or listing.

## 9. Children's Privacy
The Bot is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated as the Bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same URL.

## 11. Contact
For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Bot's owner through the support server or contact details provided with the Bot.
